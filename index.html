4C-A · DEPTH-AWARE HEDGE SIZING: The entered USDT amount is a strict maximum entry-notional ceiling per leg. Before depth loads, the engine shows a quote-based candidate. After depth loads, it reduces that candidate when necessary to the largest exact common quantity whose full market-order path is covered and whose actual VWAP entry notional does not exceed the budget on either venue. LONG quantity = SHORT quantity exactly; no sizing tolerance is used.
L3: normalized 8H funding and funding direction are preserved. 4A entry: LONG buys at venue ASK; SHORT sells at venue BID. Current exit baseline: LONG sells at BID; SHORT buys at ASK. 4C-A quote sizing first computes the largest exact common quantity step under the budget at the best quotes. Once selected-pair depth is loaded, the engine performs a discrete search over that same common step and selects the largest exact quantity for which both market-order entry paths have 100% depth coverage and both actual VWAP entry notionals are ≤ the entered budget. LONG quantity = SHORT quantity exactly. The entered USDT value is a strict ceiling, not a forced notional. Quoted 4A fees/spread remain reference-only baseline fields. 4B/4C-A is execution-authoritative and recalculates fees from consumed-book notionals, while full VWAP execution price cost already contains quoted spread plus depth slippage, so slippage is not added a second time. Matched gross funding uses the exact depth-verified common quantity when depth is available: SHORT qty × short mark × short normalized funding − LONG qty × long mark × long normalized funding. 4C-B then computes projected Net Funding / 8H only when entry and current-exit baseline depth coverage are both 100%: Gross Funding / 8H − Depth-Verified Total Execution Cost. Additional Depth Slippage remains diagnostic only and is not subtracted again.
